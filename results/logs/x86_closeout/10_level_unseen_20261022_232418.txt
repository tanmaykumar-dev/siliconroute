 [PHASE 5] ARCHITECTURAL ANALYSIS (Hypotheses & Physics Rationale)
=============================================================================================================================
Hypothesis: For MLP models, the crossover between CPU and discrete GPU (NVIDIA RTX 5070 Laptop GPU)
follows model weight footprint relative to the 64 MB CPU cache (AMD Ryzen 9 8940HX L3 cache),
rather than FLOP count.
  - MLPs possess low arithmetic intensity (~0.5 - 1.0 FLOP/byte per sample), meaning weights are streamed
    with minimal arithmetic reuse. Below 64 MB, weights fit in the CPU L3 cache, allowing
    the CPU to avoid DRAM latency and out-perform the discrete GPU, which incurs a fitted overhead of
    0.1511 ms (manifest fit_dml:1_t0_ms).
  - When MLP weights exceed 64 MB, CPU execution must stream from system DDR5 memory, allowing the
    RTX 5070 Laptop GPU (equipped with GDDR7 memory per manufacturer datasheet) to achieve lower inference latencies.
  - For Convolutional models with spatial kernel reuse, higher arithmetic intensity amortizes the 0.1511 ms
    dispatch overhead at much smaller model sizes.
