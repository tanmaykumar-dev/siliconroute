Winograd Minimal Filtering Hypothesis
Direct 3x3 2D convolution via GEMM requires 2·C_in·C_out·K_h·K_w·H·W floating point operations. Under Winograd minimal filtering algorithms (F(2x2, 3x3) or F(4x4, 3x3)), multiplication complexity is reduced by 2.25x to 4x. In DirectML, small convolution tiles are transformed to the Winograd domain, causing implied computational throughput to exceed theoretical FP32 GEMM limits on AMD Radeon 610M. These numbers represent EFFECTIVE computational throughput, not raw silicon frequency. Hardware peaks are datasheet theoretical limits, not measured.
