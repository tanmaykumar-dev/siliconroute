Privacy policy

SiliconRoute is built local-first. We do not track you, collect personal data, or transmit hardware telemetry over the network.

Local data storage only

SiliconRoute runs locally on your workstation. All latency benchmarks, energy measurements, device profiles and routing decisions are stored exclusively in a local SQLite database on your computer (`data/siliconroute.db`).

Zero cookies, analytics, or third-party requests

This website and the local dashboard load no external analytics, tracking pixels, or third-party cookies. All scripts, fonts and stylesheets are vendored locally and served from the application repository.

Contact inquiries
